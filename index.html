// the project

Three disciplines, one defence.

Phishing remains the most common entry point for breaches. SpoofLens combines protocol-level forensics, machine learning and rigorous evaluation into one transparent pipeline.

🛡️

Cybersecurity

Parses SPF, DKIM and DMARC results, Return-Path alignment and Received hops to expose spoofing, homograph domains and forged relay timestamps.

🧠

Artificial Intelligence

A gradient-boosted classifier over engineered header and URL features, with per-prediction explanations so analysts see why a message was flagged.

📊

Data Science

Class-imbalance handling, stratified cross-validation, ROC/PR analysis and feature-importance studies to measure what actually drives detection.
